## Edit Prompt

Use case: background-extraction / identity-preserve. Edit target: the attached
GEOdraft GD monogram supplied by its owner. Prepare a clean production logo
asset for a website. Remove the white background entirely, including the white
interior counterforms and diagonal gaps: output genuine transparent alpha.
Preserve the exact GD silhouette, proportions, alignment and diagonal cuts
from the reference. Preserve forest-green G (approximately #083b30) and muted
metallic-sand/gold D (approximately #b59a77), rendered as perfectly flat solid
fills, not metallic effects. Clean JPEG/photograph artifacts and make edges
crisp and smooth but DO NOT redesign the monogram. Single complete monogram,
centered with only a small 3% transparent margin, landscape composition about
2:1 width-to-height. No additional text, no GEOdraft lettering, no slogan, no
added shapes, no shadows, no gradients, no mockup, no white rectangle, no
checkerboard printed into image. Transparent background required. Save the
image as a PNG.
